Move Rate onto CH2 and use two Pico outputs to select Pitch, Amount or Rate through one ADC input. Pitch, Amount and Rate all share GP28. GP26 becomes free for a later control.

## 1. Two bits select four possibilities

A selector line can hold either `0` or `1`. With S0 alone, lesson 9 could select two channels. Adding S1 gives four combinations:

| S2 | S1 | S0 | Channel | Control |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 0 | CH0 · pin 13 | Pitch |
| 0 | 0 | 1 | CH1 · pin 14 | Amount |
| 0 | 1 | 0 | CH2 · pin 15 | Rate |
| 0 | 1 | 1 | CH3 · pin 12 | Unused |

Read the selector values as the binary number `S2 S1 S0`. CH2 is binary `010`, so S1 is high while S0 and S2 are low.

## 2. Make two changes with USB unplugged

1. Unplug the Pico's USB cable.
2. Move the **Rate centre wiper** away from `GP26` and connect it to **4051 CH2 pin 15**.
3. Move **4051 S1 pin 10** away from GND and connect it to `GP19`.
4. Keep S0 pin 11 on GP18 and S2 pin 9 on GND.
5. Keep COMMON pin 3 on GP28.
6. Keep every potentiometer's two outside legs connected to shared 3V3 and GND.
7. Keep the 4051 power, ENABLE, VEE and 100 nF capacitor exactly as in lesson 9.

<LessonTenCircuit />

:::danger Before reconnecting USB
GP26 should now be empty. Check that Rate's centre wiper reaches pin 15—not VCC pin 16 beside it. S2 pin 9 must remain on GND.
:::

### What the bit operations do

`channel & 1` keeps only the rightmost binary bit for S0. `(channel >> 1) & 1` first shifts the next bit right, then keeps it for S1.

| Channel number | Binary | S1 result | S0 result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 00 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 | 01 | 0 | 1 |
| 2 | 10 | 1 | 0 |

## 4. Test all three channels independently

1. Reconnect USB and run the code.
2. Turn Pitch. Only the printed Pitch value should change.
3. Hold Trigger and turn Amount. LED brightness should change.
4. Keep holding Trigger and turn Rate. LED flash speed should change.
5. Leave every knob still. The three printed values should remain stable apart from tiny changes.

:::details Rate works backwards
The two outside Rate wires are reversed. This is electrically safe. Unplug USB and swap those two outside wires; do not move the centre wiper.
:::

:::details Rate changes Amount or Pitch
First check Rate's centre wiper goes to CH2 pin 15. Then check S1 pin 10 reaches GP19 and S0 pin 11 still reaches GP18.
:::

:::details Rate does nothing
Unplug USB. Check that the wiper left GP26 and now reaches CH2 pin 15. Pin 15 sits directly below VCC pin 16 when the chip notch faces up.
:::
