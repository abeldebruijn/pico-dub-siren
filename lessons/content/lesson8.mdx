title: Share one Pico input between two dials
description: Move Amount onto multiplexer channel 1 and let GP18 choose between Pitch and Amount through one ADC input.

<LessonGate prerequisite="lesson-007">

Lesson 7 used the CD74HC4051 as a fixed wire: CH0 was always selected. Today one Pico output controls the lowest selector bit, `S0`. Pitch and Amount now share GP28, and the Pico switches between them in software.

## 1. The new idea

| S2 | S1 | S0 | Connected channel | Reads |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0 | 0 | CH0 · pin 13 | Pitch |
| 0 | 0 | 1 | CH1 · pin 14 | Amount |

`S1` and `S2` stay grounded, so only CH0 and CH1 are reachable. That keeps this lesson small.

## 2. Rewire with USB unplugged

1. Keep the 4051 power from lesson 7: pin 16 to 3V3, pin 8 to GND, pin 7 to GND, pin 6 to GND, capacitor between pin 16 and pin 8.
2. Keep the Pitch centre wiper on **CH0 pin 13**.
3. Move the Amount centre wiper away from `GP27` and connect it to **CH1 pin 14**.
4. Keep both outside pins of the Amount pot on shared 3V3 and GND.
5. Keep 4051 COMMON pin 3 connected to `GP28`.
6. Move **S0 pin 11** away from GND and connect it to `GP18`.
7. Keep **S1 pin 10** and **S2 pin 9** connected to GND.

<LessonEightCircuit />

Only the centre-wiper wires move through the 4051. The outside pot wires still make the 0–3.3 V range.

:::danger Before USB
Do not leave S0 floating. It must go to GP18. S1 and S2 must stay on GND. GP27 should now be empty; it is no longer reading Amount directly.
:::

## 3. Read one channel, then the other

<LessonEightCodeDiff />

### Why the tiny sleep exists

After `mux_s0.value(channel)`, the 4051 changes its internal switch. The Pico ADC input then needs a very short moment to settle to the newly selected pot voltage. `sleep(0.002)` is 2 ms; slow for a computer, but very fast for your hand turning a knob.

### Values are read directly, no smoothing

Lesson 7 already removed the exponential low-pass filter once the 100 nF bypass capacitor across the 4051's VCC/GND absorbed the ADC noise it used to hide. That capacitor is still on the breadboard, so every pot value in this lesson—Pitch and the new Amount—is assigned straight from `read_mux(...)` with no `+= (reading - value) * 0.1` averaging anywhere.

## 4. Test independence

1. Turn Pitch. The printed `Pitch` number should change. LED brightness should not.
2. Hold Trigger and turn Amount. LED brightness should change. Printed Pitch should stay nearly fixed.
3. Turn Rate. LED flash speed should change. Pitch and Amount should not swap roles.
4. Shake the breadboard lightly. Values should remain stable like your fixed lesson 7 circuit.

:::info Timing observation
Reading the mux makes the loop slower. Each loop reads two mux channels, and each read waits 2 ms for the ADC value to settle. The bottom `sleep(0.01)` adds another 10 ms, so one loop takes at least about 14 ms before Python overhead and occasional printing.

The LED does not toggle exactly every requested interval; the code only checks whether it is time to toggle once per loop. If the Rate knob asks for a 50 ms interval, the next check may happen around 56–64 ms instead.
:::

:::details Pitch and Amount are swapped
Unplug USB. Exchange only the two wiper wires going to CH0 pin 13 and CH1 pin 14.
:::

:::details Only Pitch works
Unplug USB. Recheck Amount's centre wiper to CH1 pin 14. Also check S0 pin 11 reaches GP18, not GND.
:::

:::details Values jump when untouched
Unplug USB and reseat the relevant wiper wire. Large jumps are still usually contact problems. Small one- or two-step changes are normal ADC noise.
:::
